About these videos

Every video is rendered at 256 × 256 pixels from the same 16 source views of a test scene of DL3DV, sampled uniformly from 300 consecutive frames. One source view gives 1,369 tokens, so the 16 views give 21,904.

Ours renders each frame from the source tokens that the router selects for that frame's viewpoint, at the budget shown; one ranking per frame serves every budget. No temporal smoothing is applied.

Whole view gives the decoder all tokens of the source view from which our router selects the most tokens for that frame, and Whole views the two such views (the whole view of Sec. J of the supplementary PDF). All tokens is LagerNVS, which attends to every source token.

Full trajectory is a smooth path through all 16 source cameras, at constant speed. Simulated head motion moves the camera on a circle around a viewpoint in the middle of the sequence, with its orientation fixed.

FPS is the measured rate of rendering one frame, from the viewpoint to the image, at 256 × 256 on one NVIDIA RTX PRO 5000 GPU with 16 source views.
